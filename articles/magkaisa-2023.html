Magkaisa 2023- All in the Family: Pinsans and their Non-Traditional Careers

A panel at the 2023 Magkaisa student-conference with my family I moderated

by Julian Garcia • October 23, 2023

This year, I had the honor of hosting a panel with some of my family to discuss their career paths at the 2023 Magkaisa student conference! Magkaisa (Tagalog for "unite") is an annual conference put on by the Sweetwater Union High School District for Filipino-American students, parents and educators to discuss and learn about college and career skills, as well as learn more about Filipino-American history. The event happens every year in October during Filipino-American History month.

My mom, an educator in the Sweetwater Union High School District, is one of the lead organizers of the event and has been taking me to it ever since I could remember. There's a picture of me somewhere at 6 years old sitting at the front of a classroom filled with high schoolers listening to a panelist talk about their career as a Software Engineer. Altogether, being able to be a part of this event in this way feels like a homecoming moment for me, and I'm so happy I could the chance to do it.

About the Panel

All panelists are my aunts and uncles who have all undergone and pursued non-traditional career paths. In this 40-minute conversation, we discuss what led them all to their particular careers, what they love about them, and any advice for students who want to go down a similar path.


Panelists:
  • Tara Luansing Monsod: Executive Chef at Animae, San Diego
  • Amanda Luansing: Copy Editor at UCLA Asian American Studies Center
  • Lysander Luaning Jr.: Master Instructor at Legacy Academy and Bodyworker at Stitched Up Bodyworkers
  • Nestor Luansing: Apprentice Editor at Fremantle media
  • Sheena Luansing Medina: Fashion/Wardrobe Stylist and DrJays.com and Freelance
  • Chris Luansing: Assistant Editor at Right Now Entertainment
